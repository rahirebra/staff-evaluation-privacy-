Privacy Policy — Staff Evaluation

Last updated: September 2026

Staff Evaluation is an offline app for recording daily work performance. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What data the app stores

  • The name and Staff ID you enter when signing in
  • Evaluation entries you record (dates, criteria, customer/account details you type in)
  • A profile photo, if you choose to add one

Where this data lives

All data is stored only on your device, using local browser storage inside the app. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and the app has no backend — there is no account system, cloud sync, or remote database involved.

Sharing

The app lets you export your records as a CSV or PDF file and share them using your device's own share menu (for example, to email or Telegram). This only happens when you explicitly choose to share — the app itself never sends your data anywhere automatically.

Third parties

This app does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking services.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, permanently deletes all locally stored data.
